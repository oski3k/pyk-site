A few good questions.

Do I need to give my email?

No. You can start with a nickname. Secure your account with email or Google if you want to recover it or use it on another phone, where these sign-in options are available.

Does Bluetooth mean I can play without internet?

Bluetooth finds nearby players. Matches with other people use the online service, so both phones need an internet connection. Bot practice can be played offline.

Can I play with friends farther away?

Yes. You can invite friends to a friendly match online. Nearby ranked matches and friendly matches are separate.

Which languages are in the app?

English, Polish, Ukrainian, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese. You can change the language in your profile.

Where can I get help?

Email hello@oskarsledz.pl. For an account deletion request, use the instructions on our Delete account page.

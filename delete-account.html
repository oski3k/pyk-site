How to delete your Pyk account

Pyk app, developer: Oskar Śledź.

In the app

  1. Open the Pyk app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are gone immediately.

Without the app

Write to hello@oskarsledz.pl with the subject “Delete my Pyk account” and give your @handle. If your account is secured with e-mail or Google, write from that address. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm it in our reply.

What we delete

  • profile: nickname, @handle, profile photo, items you wear,
  • sign-in data: e-mail, password (hash) and the link to your Google account,
  • rating, number of games and wins, and your part of the match history,
  • friend list, invites, pokes and referrals,
  • coins, items bought and purchase history (also in RevenueCat),
  • notification tokens, Bluetooth tokens and online status,
  • usage statistics stored in our database.

What stays and for how long

  • Matches you played stay with your opponents, no longer linked to you (they are part of their ranking). We delete them after 12 months at the latest.
  • The log of sent notifications disappears after 7 days.
  • Google Analytics statistics are not linked to your account and disappear after 2 months.
  • Server technical logs usually disappear after 7 days.
  • Google Play or the App Store keep payment data under their own terms.

Pyk+ subscription: deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or the App Store. Coins and items are lost together with the account.

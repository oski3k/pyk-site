Pyk Privacy Policy

Effective from 30 September 2026

Pyk is an app with short mini-games for people who are next to each other. We only collect the data the game needs to work, plus a few usage statistics that help us improve it. We do not show ads and we do not sell data. Below we explain what data we process, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how to delete it.

1. Controller and contact

The controller of your personal data is Oskar Śledź, the developer of Pyk (Poland). For anything related to privacy, write to hello@oskarsledz.pl. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as the law does not require one.

2. In short

  • You can play without giving us an e-mail address. You secure your account (Google, or e-mail and password) only if you want to.
  • Other players see your nickname, @handle, profile photo and rating. They do not see your e-mail.
  • Over Bluetooth your phone broadcasts only a random token. We do not collect GPS location.
  • We collect app usage statistics (also through Google Analytics for Firebase), without the advertising ID.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app: Profile → Delete account.

Providing data is voluntary, but you cannot play without a nickname and an account ID. E-mail, photo, Google sign-in, notifications and purchases are optional.

Data that stays on your phone

The app stores on your phone, among other things, your sign-in session, chosen language, personal bests in games and which pop-ups you have already seen. This data does not go to our server and disappears when you uninstall the app.

4. What other players see

  • Any signed-in player who sees your profile (e.g. when you are next to each other, in the ranking or after searching your @handle): nickname, @handle, profile photo, rating, number of games and wins, items you wear and the Pyk+ badge.
  • Your friends also see whether you are in the app right now.
  • A person you send an invite or a poke to gets a notification with your nickname.
  • Your QR code contains only your @handle.

We never show anyone your e-mail, coin balance, purchases or usage statistics.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect GPS location and do not determine where exactly you are.
  • We do not collect your phone number or contacts. From your gallery we only take the one photo you choose (resized to 256 pixels).
  • We do not send a list of nearby Bluetooth devices to the server.
  • We do not show ads, do not read the advertising ID, and do not sell or share data for advertising.
  • We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects. The rating is calculated automatically from match results, but it is only part of the game.

6. Bluetooth and phone permissions

Bluetooth / Nearby devices is used to discover players next to you. Your phone broadcasts only a random token that changes every time the app starts. We never broadcast your nickname, ID or phone name. The token means nothing on its own. The server reveals who is behind it only to signed-in players and only for 15 minutes.

Location (Android 11 and older only): the system requires this permission to scan for Bluetooth. We do not use it to determine where you are.

Camera is used only to scan a friend’s QR code. The image never leaves your phone.

Photos: we only use the photo you choose as your profile photo.

Notifications: we ask once. You can change your choice any time in your phone settings.

7. Who we share data with

We use companies that process data on our behalf (processors) or run a feature you choose:

  • Supabase, Inc. – server, database, sign-in, photo storage. Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland, an Amazon Web Services data centre).
  • Google (Google Ireland Limited, Google LLC) – Google Analytics for Firebase (statistics), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Google sign-in (if you choose it), Google Play (payments, app updates).
  • Apple Inc. – on iPhones: delivering notifications (APNs) and App Store payments.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) – verifying purchases and subscriptions. It receives your random account ID, purchase data and basic device and app data (e.g. IP address, phone model, operating system, app version, store country). It does not receive your nickname or e-mail. When you delete your account we also delete you from RevenueCat.

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store as the seller. We have no access to your card or bank details. We may also disclose data to public authorities where the law requires it.

8. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Google, Apple, RevenueCat and Supabase may process some data in the United States or other countries outside the EEA. In that case the data is protected by a European Commission adequacy decision (e.g. the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the recipient is certified) or by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. You can get a copy of these safeguards by writing to the address in section 1.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it,
  • correct your data (you can change your nickname, @handle and photo yourself in your profile),
  • erase your data (yourself in the app or by e-mail, section 10),
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability for the data you gave us,
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, including usage statistics,
  • withdraw consent at any time (e.g. for notifications, in your phone settings), without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing,
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in the country where you live or work.

To use your rights, write to the address in section 1. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours (e.g. by writing from the address linked to the account or by giving your @handle). Google Analytics statistics are not linked to your account, so we cannot tell which of them are yours. They disappear after the retention period.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. We then immediately delete your profile, photo, sign-in data, friends, coins, purchases, notification tokens and usage statistics in our database. Details and how to do it without the app: how to delete your account.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pyk+ subscription in the store. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

11. Security

Connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Database access rules make sure every player sees only what they should. Passwords are stored only as a hash. No protection is perfect, though. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the supervisory authority as the law requires.

12. Age

The app is intended for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from younger people. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete it. A parent or guardian can write to the address in section 1.

13. Information for residents of specific countries

United Kingdom and Switzerland. We process your data in line with the UK GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and you have the same rights as in section 9. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or the Swiss FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch).

United States (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and other states with privacy laws). In the last 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (nickname, @handle, account ID, e-mail, device and installation IDs, IP address), commercial information (purchases), app activity (usage statistics, match history), approximate country and region derived from the IP address, and your profile photo if you add one. Sections 3 and 7 describe the sources, purposes and recipients. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising (as defined by the CCPA/CPRA), we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we have no actual knowledge of collecting data from anyone under 16. You have the right to know what we collect, to correct it, to delete it and to opt out of its sale or sharing (which we do not do). We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. You or an authorised agent can send a request to the address in section 1. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our message.

Brazil. You have the rights under Art. 18 of the LGPD (including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, deletion, portability, information about recipients and withdrawal of consent). You can complain to the ANPD (gov.br/anpd).

Other countries. If the law of your country gives you additional rights, write to the address in section 1 and we will help you use them.

14. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will tell you about important changes in the app. The current version is always at this address, and the date at the top shows when it took effect.

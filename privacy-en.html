03What other players can see

Players who can view your profile can see your nickname, @handle, avatar or photo, rating, game statistics, cosmetics and Pyk+ badge. Nearby discovery and player search can expose this profile to people who are not your friends. Your email and sign-in details are not displayed to them.

Friend relationships are available to the two people involved. Online status and daily challenge standings are limited to current friends. Duel balance is available to the participants while they are current, unblocked friends. Your opponent receives the gameplay information needed for a match. Your block list and reports are not a public feed.

04Bluetooth & phone permissions

Bluetooth: a random 8-byte token is advertised, without your nickname, account ID or phone name. Nearby scans stay on your phone; the app asks the server to resolve Pyk tokens, rather than uploading a list of surrounding devices. The token is pseudonymous, not anonymous: the server can link an active token to your profile, and an observer can recognise the same token during a session.

Location: Pyk does not collect GPS coordinates. Android 11 and earlier may require location permission for Bluetooth scanning; we do not use it to determine your position. Network providers and Firebase can process IP addresses, including to infer an approximate country.

Camera: used to scan a friend's QR code; camera frames are processed on the phone and not uploaded. Photos: only a selected profile photo is uploaded. Notifications: managed through your phone settings. Denying a permission disables its related feature, rather than granting unrelated data access.

We do not access your contacts or phone number, show ads, sell personal data, or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

05Why processing is permitted

For users in the EEA/UK, our bases are: performance of the service contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) for accounts, gameplay, friends, rewards and purchases; legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in security, fraud prevention, fair rankings, support and proportionate service statistics, subject to your rights; and legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)) where records or disclosure are legally required. Where consent is required for non-essential device storage/access or analytics, that processing requires consent (Article 6(1)(a)); accepting this notice or an operating-system permission is not an analytics consent.

The current app sends usage events automatically when the online backend and Firebase are configured and does not offer a dedicated analytics consent switch. You may contact us to object or request deletion; an email request does not itself switch off collection on your device. This notice does not replace any consent controls required by local law.

Essential account and gameplay data are needed to provide online play. Email sign-in, profile photos, feedback and purchases are optional. We do not use automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects; game ratings, scores and queue pairing are calculated automatically to operate the games.

06Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, database, storage and real-time gameplay. The production project is in the EU West region, Ireland.
  • Google / Firebase: app analytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging when configured; Google also handles optional Google sign-in and Google Play purchases.
  • Apple: APNs push delivery and App Store purchases where available.
  • RevenueCat: purchase/subscription validation and entitlements when purchases are enabled.
  • Web3Forms and our email provider: feedback delivery and support correspondence.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting this website, including connection/security data.

Authorised administrators and moderators can access data needed for support, operations and safety. We may disclose data where legally required. App stores and sign-in providers also process information under their own terms; their independent records are not controlled by Pyk.

Their service-specific transfer terms and safeguards apply; contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your data.

Regional rights depend on the law's scope; availability in a country does not mean that every privacy statute applies to Pyk.

08Your choices & requests

Write to hello@oskarsledz.pl with your request and @handle. If your account is secured, use the linked email where possible. We may request proportionate proof that you control the account; never send us your password, one-time sign-in code or payment card details. An authorised representative may contact us where local law permits.

We respond within the time required by applicable law, normally within one month for GDPR requests; if a lawful extension is needed, we explain it. If we cannot fulfil a request, we explain why and the available complaint or appeal route. You can edit profile details in the app, remove a profile photo, manage permissions in phone settings, remove friends, block players and request account deletion.

09Deletion & remaining records

Use Profile → Delete account, or follow our account deletion instructions without the app. This removes your authentication account, profile and account-linked server data, including friends, rewards, analytics events, push tokens, attempts, blocks/reports and duel histories. Photos are removed from Pyk storage as part of the app's deletion flow.

A match record and another participant's own data may remain until the 12-month limit. Pyk attempts to delete the RevenueCat subscriber through its server; this third-party step can fail independently and may need follow-up. Firebase events use a separate installation ID and are not automatically erased by deleting your Pyk account. Contact us about provider-held data; deletion depends on the identifiers available and the provider's process.

Feedback emails and providers' security, backup or legally required records are not automatically removed by the app action. Their applicable retention and deletion processes still apply. Deleting Pyk does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it in the relevant store. Removing the app alone does not delete the server account.

10Age & young players

Pyk is intended for people aged 16 or older, or the higher minimum required by local law. It is not directed to children under 16, and does not offer a parental-consent flow or collect a date of birth for age verification. A store age/content rating does not override this rule. If local law requires a parent or guardian's involvement for a minor, that requirement still applies.

If you believe a child below the permitted age has provided personal data, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate. We do not knowingly collect data from children below the permitted age.

11Website, storage & security

The public home, privacy and deletion pages do not embed analytics, advertising, external fonts or trackers, and do not set cookies or browser storage. GitHub Pages processes connection information such as IP addresses and request metadata under its own privacy statement. Following store or provider links takes you to their services.

The separate administrator panel uses Supabase authentication and browser storage for an administrator's session; it is not required to read these public pages. The mobile app stores account/session settings and pending daily results locally. We use encrypted network connections and server access controls; no system can guarantee absolute security.

This notice describes the features currently implemented. Server-controlled or optional features may be unavailable in a particular build. It does not imply that disabled social invitation links or private user-created challenges are active.
